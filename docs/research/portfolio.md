# GenZero: portfolio, competitors and keywords (T0.6)

Researched 2026-10-14 by the `researcher` agent from public sources. Every row cites a URL.
"Verified" means the claim appears on the cited page, which was fetched on 2026-10-14. "Unverified" means it came from
a single secondary or paywalled source, or a search summary only.

**Primary source:** https://genzero.co/portfolio/ (HTTP 200, fetched with curl). All 27 names listed under
"Our Portfolio and Partners" were confirmed in the raw HTML.

**Context:** GenZero was founded by Temasek in 2022 with an initial S$5bn commitment
([Eco-Business, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.eco-business.com/news/temaseks-genzero-reports-47-rise-in-direct-climate-impact-as-portfolio-expands/)).
In FY to Mar 2026 it reported 26 closed investments across 26 countries and 1.4 MtCO2e of direct impact in 2025.
It targets 7 MtCO2e of cumulative direct impact by Mar 2028 (same source).

## 1. Focus areas (investment pillars)

Source: https://genzero.co/portfolio/

| Pillar | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Nature-based Solutions** | Forestry, landscape restoration and agricultural decarbonisation (e.g. rice methane). Covers direct investments, programme partners (carbon projects) and forestry funds. |
| **Technology-based Solutions** | Climate tech such as carbon removal (biochar), sustainable fuels (SAF), low-carbon materials (cement, bioplastics) and renewable energy platforms. Includes LP stakes in climate VC/PE funds. |
| **Carbon Ecosystem Enablers** | Carbon-market infrastructure: ratings, exchanges and marketplaces, MRV (measurement, reporting, verification) and project developers. Also market-building initiatives such as transition credits, SAF certificates and CORSIA/Article 6. |

Stated 2026 strategy shift: a "focus on commercially viable climate solutions" and aligning projects with buyer demand and compliance signals
([GenZero, 3 Aug 2026](https://genzero.co/genzero-reports-1-4-mtco2e-increase-in-cumulative-climate-impact-as-it-deepens-focus-on-commercially-viable-climate-solutions/)).
A Carbon Pulse report says GenZero adapted its strategy "amid VCM contraction"
([Carbon Pulse, paywalled; detail unverified](https://carbon-pulse.com/537875/)).

## 4. Sector keywords (for news pre-filtering)

Use these as case-insensitive substring matches on title and summary. They are grouped by pillar here but form one flat list in the YAML.

- **Nature:** reforestation, afforestation, forestry, nature-based solutions, landscape restoration, improved forest management, REDD+, mangrove, soil carbon, regenerative agriculture, rice methane, biodiversity credits
- **Tech:** carbon removal, direct air capture, biochar, carbon capture, CCUS, sustainable aviation fuel, SAF, e-fuels, green hydrogen, low-carbon cement, green steel, bioplastic, battery storage, solar, renewable energy, energy transition
- **Carbon ecosystem:** carbon credits, carbon market, voluntary carbon market, compliance carbon market, carbon offset, carbon rating, carbon exchange, MRV, Article 6, CORSIA, transition credits, coal phase-out, net zero
- **Deal signals:** climate tech funding, Series A, decarbonisation, decarbonization

Notes: short tokens like "SAF" and "MRV" should be whole-word matches to avoid false hits. Include both UK and US spellings
(decarbonisation/decarbonization).

## 5. Gaps and open questions

- GenZero reports **26 closed investments**, but the public page lists 27 entities, including programme partners that may not count as investments. The exact mapping is not public.
- Cheque sizes and stakes are undisclosed for almost all entries.
- genzero.co announcement pages 2 and 3 failed to paginate when fetched, so pre-2026 GenZero press releases were reconstructed from news coverage. Some 2022–2025 partnerships may be missing.
- The Nikkei / Mitsubishi carbon-credit buyers' body and GenZero's role in it are unverified.
- Competitor fund sizes marked "unverified" come from single press reports.
- D5 (CEO/CIO needs) is still open. The competitor list is a researcher judgement, and the user should confirm which peers matter most.
